# Privacy policy: Ankh Do

Ankh Do (the GNOME Shell extension and the browser extension) does not
collect, use, store remotely, sell, or share any personal data.

- **Your tasks stay on your device.** They are saved locally: in your
  browser's extension storage, or in `~/.config/scroll-of-deeds/` on GNOME.
- **No network access.** The extension makes no network requests, has no
  analytics, and contains no tracking.
- **Clipboard.** It writes to the clipboard only when you copy a task, and
  never reads from it.
- **Permissions.** The browser extension requests only `storage`, used to
  save your tasks and your theme choice on your device.

Uninstalling the extension removes its browser storage. On GNOME, delete
`~/.config/scroll-of-deeds/` to remove your tasks.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/TheSant0x/AnkhDo/issues>.

Last updated: October 2026.
