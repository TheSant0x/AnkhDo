game-icons.net and are licensed under Creative Commons BY 3.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Modifications: opaque background removed, recolored for GNOME symbolic
recoloring, renamed to the *-symbolic.svg convention; the ankh is rotated
45° (and scaled to fit) so it stands upright.
